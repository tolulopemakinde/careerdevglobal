## 2. Internationally Mobile Professionals and Migrants — Day 13

**Campaign name:** CDG PRE-LAUNCH | International Mobility | Day 13  
**Subject:** Prepare your career move with clear expectations  
**Preheader:** Make your experience understandable across markets—and know what career support can and cannot do.

**Email body:**

Hello,

Moving into a new country or labour market can mean explaining familiar experience in a different professional context. Job titles, qualifications, terminology, and employer expectations may vary, so it helps to make your skills and achievements clear without changing the facts.

A practical starting point is to:

- Identify the roles and sectors relevant to your target market.
- Compare your experience with current, credible role requirements.
- Describe transferable skills and achievements accurately.
- Adapt your CV and professional profile to the audience.
- Verify important requirements with authoritative sources before making decisions.

CareerDev Global supports career preparation, professional positioning, learning, and coaching. It does not make immigration decisions, provide a visa or employment guarantee, or replace qualified immigration/legal advice. Requirements can change, so use current official sources for immigration and work-authorisation questions.
