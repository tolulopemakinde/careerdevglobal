## Editorial standards

- Use credible, current sources for factual claims and cite them.
- Never fabricate statistics, case studies, testimonials, credentials, or client results.
- Do not promise a job, interview, visa, promotion, or other outcome outside the service's control.
- Use consented, anonymized client examples only.
- Avoid generic engagement bait, pressure, fake scarcity, and unsolicited bulk messaging.
- Use a clear, relevant next step; not every post needs a direct sales pitch.
- Check mobile readability, accessibility, spelling, and all links before publication.
- Review results using qualified visits, leads, conversions, paid orders and contribution—not engagement alone.
